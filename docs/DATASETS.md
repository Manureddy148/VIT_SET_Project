Pima remains a supported future adapter, not this run's dataset. No claim that CDC fields (survey buckets, HighBP, BMI, etc.) are glucose/insulin measurements. No claim these labels measure severity. No credentialed datasets or restricted patient records were used.

Raw datasets, checkpoints and abstract corpus stay outside Git. Dataset hashes are in `reports/*_metrics.json`. Downloading a dataset is not authority to publish its contents. Never put actual patient data in committed audit logs.
