Cardiac · Electrophysiology

ECG — Electrocardiogram

Time-series electrical activity of the heart. 12-lead ECG remains the gold standard for arrhythmia detection, MI diagnosis, and cardiac risk stratification. AI models analyse waveform morphology across leads simultaneously.

📈
AI-Ready Waveform 12-Lead
12-Lead Standard ECG
RESTING · 10-SECOND · 500 HZ STANDARD
Gold standard cardiac recording. Captures P wave, QRS complex, T wave across 12 anatomical leads. Critical for diagnosing STEMI, NSTEMI, bundle branch blocks, LVH, Wolff-Parkinson-White syndrome.
File formats .dicom (waveform), .xml (HL7 aECG), .csv, .mat, .dat+.hea (PhysioNet)
Sampling 250–1000 Hz, 12 leads, 10 sec standard; 30 min for Holter
Key signals QRS duration, QTc interval, PR interval, ST deviation, T-wave inversion, axis
Diseases STEMI, NSTEMI, AF, VT, complete HB, hypertrophy, WPW, QT prolongation

Cardiac · Structural

Echocardiography

Ultrasound imaging of cardiac structure and function. Transthoracic (TTE), Transesophageal (TEE), and Stress Echo. AI measures ejection fraction, wall motion, valve areas, and detects cardiomyopathies automatically.

🎬
Video DICOM TTE/TEE
Transthoracic Echocardiography (TTE)
2D · M-MODE · DOPPLER · COLOR FLOW
Standard non-invasive cardiac ultrasound. Provides real-time video of cardiac chambers, valves, and wall motion. AI models measure EF (left ventricular ejection fraction), GLS (global longitudinal strain), and classify cardiomyopathies.
File format DICOM multiframe (.dcm), AVI, MP4 (30–150 fps, 112×112 to 608×452)
Views Parasternal long/short axis, Apical 4/2/3-chamber, Subcostal
Key metrics EF%, LVEDV, LVESV, LV mass, E/A ratio, TAPSE, PAP
AI targets EF estimation, HCM/DCM classification, AS severity, TR/MR grading
echonet-dynamic/echonet-dynamic (Stanford)

🔗 Pipeline Integration

Stanford EchoNet-Dynamic outputs EF% directly on a 0–100 scale, making it trivial to map: severity = 100 - EF_percent for systolic dysfunction severity.

🌊
Spectral Doppler
Doppler Echocardiography
PW · CW · TISSUE · COLOR FLOW MAPPING
Velocity-based assessment of blood and tissue motion. Pulsed Wave (PW) for mitral inflow patterns, Continuous Wave (CW) for valve gradient, Tissue Doppler Imaging (TDI) for diastolic function. Critical for heart failure with preserved EF (HFpEF) diagnosis.
PW metrics E velocity, A velocity, E/A ratio, DT (deceleration time)
TDI metrics e', a', s' velocities, E/e' ratio (filling pressure proxy)
CW metrics Peak gradient, mean gradient, AVA (aortic valve area)
Severity map E/e' <8 = normal; 8–14 = intermediate; >14 = elevated filling pressure
💉
TEE 3D Echo
TEE & 3D Echocardiography
TRANS-ESOPHAGEAL · VOLUMETRIC
Transesophageal echo provides superior image quality for valve disease, endocarditis, and intracardiac thrombus. 3D echo enables volumetric EF calculation and mitral valve anatomy for surgical planning.
3D Formats DICOM volumetric, proprietary (Philips QLAB, GE EchoPAC)
AI advantage Automated 3D EF (no geometric assumptions), mitral annulus quantification

Sonography / General Ultrasound

File format DICOM (.dcm), JPEG still frames, AVI/MP4 cineloops
Liver AI Steatosis grading (S0–S3), fibrosis (F0–F4), focal lesion classification
Kidney AI Hydronephrosis grading (G0–G4), stone detection, cyst classification (Bosniak)
Gallbladder Stone vs polyp vs sludge, wall thickening (cholecystitis), polyp size

Neurophysiology · Signals

EEG & Neurophysiology

Electrical brain activity recorded from scalp electrodes. EEG, EMG, nerve conduction studies, evoked potentials. Critical for epilepsy, sleep staging, anaesthesia depth monitoring, and BCI applications.

🧠
EEGTime-Series
EEG — Electroencephalogram
10-20 SYSTEM · 19–256 CHANNELS · 256–2048 HZ
Multichannel brain electrical activity. AI detects seizure patterns (ictal/interictal), classifies sleep stages (N1/N2/N3/REM), estimates anaesthesia depth (BIS equivalent), and diagnoses encephalopathy.
File formats EDF/EDF+ (.edf), BDF (.bdf), EEGLAB (.set), BrainVision (.vhdr/.vmrk/.eeg)
Bands Delta (0.5–4Hz), Theta (4–8Hz), Alpha (8–13Hz), Beta (13–30Hz), Gamma (30+Hz)
AI tasks Seizure detection (sensitivity 95%+), sleep staging, spike/sharp-wave classify
Severity map Seizure burden (%/hour), NCSE detection, burst-suppression ratio
ChaoZhang/EEG-Sleep-Stage-Scoring
Raw EDF → Bandpass → Artifact ICA → Spectrogram → CNN/LSTM → Score

EMGNCS
EMG & Nerve Conduction Studies
NEEDLE EMG · SURFACE EMG · NCS · BERA
Peripheral nervous system assessment. Motor and sensory nerve conduction velocities, F-wave, H-reflex, needle EMG for motor unit analysis. AI classifies neuropathy vs myopathy vs normal, localises lesion level.
NCS metrics Conduction velocity (m/s), CMAP amplitude (mV), DML (ms), latency
